# Evaluating the Effects of Reclaimed Water on the Physical properties of soils in recharge basins

## Project Overview
Reclaimed water is commonly used in recharge basins to supplement groundwater supplies, especially in arid regions. Reclaimed water is treated wastewater, and the level of treatment can significantly alter its chemical composition. This project evaluates how different levels of reclaimed water treatment affect soil physical properties, specifically infiltration and evaporation.


## Objective
The goal of this project is to determine how varying levels of water treatment influence soil behavior in recharge systems. Three types of water are compared:
- Untreated effluent (baseline reclaimed water)
- Ultrafiltration-treated water
- Reverse osmosis-treated water (after ultrafiltration)

## Methods
This study uses laboratory soil tests to measure infiltration rates and evaporation under different water treatment conditions. By comparing these treatments, we can assess how changes in water chemistry impact soil performance.
